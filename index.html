The problem

Showing up is easy — proving you showed up is the hard part.

Every school in Panama asks its students for volunteer hours. Almost none of them have a way to track them.

    By the time it matters — an application, a graduation requirement — the record is a pile of paper and a guess.

    Volunet01
    Chapter one What it is

    The whole record in one place.

    Your school approves the organizations once. They post what they need. You apply, you show up, and the hours are signed and verified before they ever reach your record.
